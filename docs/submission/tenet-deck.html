02 · Why nowrelation to category 20 · idea & innovation 30

Agents got hands. Authorization did not change.

  • The tools shipped first. Tool-calling agents moved into production systems, and every one of them arrives holding a credential — a password, not an order. MCP gives transport and a consent *framework*; its own spec tells implementers to build the authorization flows themselves.
  • The regulator is already here. DORA has been in force since 17 January 2025 and covers 22,000+ financial entities and ICT providers in the EU — all of them must now *prove* digital operational resilience, not describe it.
  • So the question stopped being technical. It is now: under whose authority did that action happen, and can you show it on demand? Nothing on the shelf answers with a stop, not a log.

04 · The solutionidea & innovation 30 · relation to category 20

No approval, no action. TENET holds that one rule.

Every tool-call passes the kernel before it executes: scope, TTL, signer. One click takes the authority back — and the agent stops mid-flight, not after the export.

Authority
Identity is bound to one agent and one grant. A grant for one order never authorizes another.
Grant
HMAC-SHA256 over the scope. The signature is verified at the gate, before any rule is read.
Revoke
One call halts the agent mid-flight. The next call returns REVOKED, not “unknown”.
Receipt
Append-only, hash-chained proof: who authorized, under which order, how long it lived.
  • Every call is either rejected before execution or carries a receipt.

05 · The product — how it workspractical applicability 20 · completeness & implementation 10

A control layer with a door — and it opens only for you.

1you say what the agent should do — typed or spoken
2your AI proposes one concrete action and says what it will touch
3the kernel decides: allowed, not allowed, or waiting for you
4you decide — and only your decision moves it; nothing runs on its own
5the action is witnessed crossing the line (or stopped at it)
6you get the receipt — a real record from a real round
7WIN: the agent acted inside the line you drew

Embedding it is one hop: POST /mcp in front of the existing tool server — no rewrite of the agent, no rewrite of the tools.
